# Two-clone comparisons isolating a large 2p or 6p gain

Generated by `src/find_clean_2p_6p_clone_pairs.R` on 2026-08-27, against the
majority-of-rounds numbat objects (job 11396137). Companion to
[`rb_2p_6p_gain_samples.md`](rb_2p_6p_gain_samples.md), which establishes *which
samples* carry the gains; this asks *which clone pair within a sample* isolates
one.

## Criteria

For attributing expression differences to a single SCNA, a pair of clones must
differ by the 2p/6p gain and by as little else as possible.

1. **Clean** — few other segments differ. `n_other_seg` counts differing
   segments outside the focal gain; `n_other_chrom` counts the chromosomes they
   sit on, which is the better proxy for "many other SCNAs" because one arm-level
   event is often split across several consecutive segments.
2. **Large** — focal gain >= 20 Mb inside the arm window.
3. **MYCN** — 2p gains must overlap chr2:15,940,550-15,947,007 (hg38). Applied to
   2p only; for 6p the column is `NA`, not `FALSE`.
4. **Viable** — >= 20 cells in each clone, or no diffex is possible.

Two further columns decide usability and are the reason the naive ranking is
wrong:

- **`whole_chrom`** — whether the focal segment runs well past the centromere. A
  "2p gain" whose segment spans 0.2-170 Mb is a whole-chromosome-2 gain: 2q rides
  along and cannot be separated from 2p/MYCN by that comparison.
- **`support`** — the sample-level confidence tier from the companion document.

`SRX10264518` is the best all-round 2p case: a genuine 80 Mb 2p-restricted gain,
strong support, both clones large, and the three other changes are deletions on
chromosomes 3 and 6 rather than a second canonical RB event.

`SRX10264523` is the cleanest by chromosome count — clones 4 and 7 differ *only*
on chromosome 2 — and has by far the largest clones. But its 2p segment runs
0.2-170.0 Mb and the one other change is 2d (174.2-235.9 Mb), so the pair really
contrasts whole-chr2 gain against diploid. Use it if a chromosome-2 dosage
contrast is acceptable; not if 2p must be separated from 2q.

**Not recommended, despite ranking first mechanically:** `SRX14116946` 2 -> 13
is the only 2p pair with *zero* other differing segments, but its 2p call is the
`weak` one — a single whole-chr2 `bamp` segment at LLR 5.1 present in 6.7% of
cells. See the caveat below.

`SRX14116944` 2 -> 3 is the strongest case in either arm: essentially the entire

`SRX10831281` is equally clean but clone 1 has only 37 cells.

Two to avoid for attribution:
- `SRX14116946` 4 -> 7 looks clean (1 other change) but the focal segment is
  110.9 Mb — a whole-chromosome-6 gain, so 6q is confounded.
- `SRX14116947` 1 -> 2 carries a 2p gain in the *same* transition, so it cannot
  separate 6p from 2p. It is the same pair that ranks 6th for 2p, for the same
  reason.
- `SRX11133588` 2 -> 5 has zero other differing segments but is the `weak` 6p
  call (LLR 7.6, 16.8% of cells) with only 29 and 66 cells.

## The cleanest pairs are the least trustworthy calls

Both pairs in the cohort with `n_other_seg == 0` — `SRX14116946` 2p and
`SRX11133588` 6p — are exactly the two calls flagged `weak` in the companion
document. This is not a coincidence and is worth stating plainly: an isolated,
low-LLR, low-cell-fraction segment is *more* likely to appear as a lone
difference between two clones precisely because it is not part of a real clonal
expansion, which typically carries several events. **Ranking on cleanliness alone
selects for artefacts.** Cleanliness has to be read together with the support
tier, which is why `support` is joined into the recommendation tables above.

## Scope and caveats

- 36 SRX samples; `SRX11133592/93/94` are held out per `AGENTS.md`.
- 9 samples yield a viable 2p pair and 8 a viable 6p pair, out of 13 and 16
  carrying the gain. The rest lose their pairs to the 20-cell floor or, for 6p,
  the 20 Mb size floor (15 pairs).
- Both nested (ancestor -> descendant) and sibling pairs are scanned; every
  recommendation above happens to be nested.
- Clone genotypes come from `GT_opt` in `clone_post`, whose segment labels match
  **`joint_post`, not `segs_consensus`** — in SRX10031193 round 2 the GT segments
  2f/2i/2m/6c exist only in `joint_post`, where `segs_consensus` calls the same
  region 2e/2g/2k. Joining GT labels to `segs_consensus` silently drops most of
  the genotype. Coordinates here come from `joint_post`.
- These are numbat clone calls, independent of the hand-curated
  `large_clone_comparisons` in `R/pipeline_targets_integration.R:227-242`.
  Reconciling the two is a separate decision.

## Reproducing

```bash
module purge && module load gcc/13.3.0 r/4.4.1
export LD_LIBRARY_PATH="$HOME/lib${LD_LIBRARY_PATH:+:$LD_LIBRARY_PATH}"
Rscript src/find_clean_2p_6p_clone_pairs.R
```

Outputs `results/clone_pairs_2p_6p_all.csv` (300 candidate pairs),
`results/clone_pairs_2p_6p_clean.csv` (216 passing viability/size/MYCN) and
`results/clone_pairs_2p_6p_best_per_sample.csv` (the 17 rows above). Read-only
with respect to `output/numbat_sridhar/`.
