## Figure numbers

When looking up or citing a manuscript figure or table number, use
`docs/figure_numbering_crosswalk.md`. It maps the draft's captions, its in-text citations,
and the `figure_order` labels in `R/pipeline_constants.R`. Supplementary figures were
renumbered S1-S30 by first citation on 2026-10-04, so older notes and thesis numbers
(e.g. "Fig. S24", "Fig 4.9E") may be stale.
